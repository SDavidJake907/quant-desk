The last scan, end to end

Every 30 minutes the scanner starts from the whole universe and narrows it down. Each bar is how many names survived that step, on a log scale so the last steps stay visible; the reasons below a step are why the rest were refused.

Open positions

Live stop and target are read from the broker, not from what was intended. The bar under each position runs from stop to target; the dot is where price is now.

Waiting

Orders sent but not filled, and setups deferred until a blocker clears.

Gemma

Gemma reads the news. It can refuse a trade through the news gate but never finds, prices or sizes one. Its ranking is recorded, not obeyed, until the scoreboard shows its strong calls beat its weak ones.

Scoreboard, closed trades

A call is right when strong ended in profit or weak ended in a loss. Neutral is not a call. Decided by the outcome, not by Gemma.
